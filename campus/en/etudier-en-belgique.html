Why Belgium?

An excellent destination at the heart of Europe

Belgium combines quality education, reasonable fees and a dynamic student life in a French-speaking and international setting.

Recognized and affordable education

Diplomas recognized across Europe with some of the most affordable tuition fees in Europe, including in renowned high schools and universities.

Heart of Europe

Located at the center of Europe, Belgium offers easy access to major European capitals and a dynamic international job market.

French-speaking setting

Brussels and Wallonia welcome you in a French-speaking environment, ideal for quickly succeeding in your studies without a language barrier.

International atmosphere

A cosmopolitan student community and multicultural campuses that prepare you for a truly European and global career.

Internship opportunities

Many programs include company internships, allowing you to build your professional network from your studies.

Dynamic student life

Lively cities, a rich student life and controlled living costs for an excellent balance between studies and leisure.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most asked questions by our candidates for Belgium.

Yes, for a stay of more than 90 days, you must obtain a long-stay student visa as well as a residence permit. We handle this entire procedure, from preparing your file to following up with the authorities.

Enrollment fees in Belgium are among the most affordable in Europe, especially in public universities and high schools. They vary by type of institution and program. Our advisors prepare a complete realistic budget for you: tuition, accommodation, insurance and everyday living.

Yes, an international student can carry out paid activity within a certain time limit. This allows you to fund part of your stay and gain a first professional experience in Belgium. We advise you on the conditions to respect.

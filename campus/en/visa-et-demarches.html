Types of visas

The right visa for your project

Depending on your goal, a suitable visa allows you to stay, study or work legally in Europe.

Student visa

To pursue higher education in France or Belgium. It authorizes full-time studies and part-time student work.

Work / salaried visa

To take up a salaried job in an in-demand occupation, as part of a legal, supervised migration to Europe.

VLS-TS

The long-stay visa equivalent to a residence permit: a simplified procedure for stays of more than 3 months, especially for studies.

Short stay

For a tourist, business or family stay of less than 90 days in the Schengen area.

Residence permit

To regularize and extend your stay once you are there, depending on your situation (studies, work, family reunification).

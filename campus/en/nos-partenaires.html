Campus Inter represents several groups of European institutions in several African countries. Among more than a hundred institutions located in several European cities, you will certainly find the ideal pathway for your academic project.

Galileo Global EducationLargest private group in Europe

Galileo Global Education

Campus Inter is a partner of the largest private higher education group in Europe. This group brings together more than 70 schools and universities across 20 countries, training more than 300,000 students every year in fields as varied as management, digital, communication, arts, health and educational innovation.

Thanks to this network, our candidates have access to international pathways, recognized professional diplomas and strong employability internationally.

Complete support

Financial solutions and secure accommodation

Beyond studies and employment, we make your funding and settlement in France easier.

Financial partners

To make your study or professional migration project feasible and secure, Campus Inter works with specialized financial partners offering:

  • adapted student loan solutions
  • targeted scholarships and financial aid
  • personalized advice to manage your budget

These partners are selected for their reliability, flexibility and transparency, to make it easier for you to access training and life in France.

Housing partners

We also put a network of housing partners at your disposal so your arrival in France is comfortable and peaceful:

  • partner student residences
  • temporary or long-stay accommodation solutions
  • support in finding accommodation according to your budget

A network of reliable housing options to arrive with peace of mind and focus on what matters.

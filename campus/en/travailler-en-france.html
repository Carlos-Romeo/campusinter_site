Work visa FAQ

Your questions about working in France

Answers to the most common questions about professional migration to France.

Yes, for most work visas you need a job offer from a French employer. That's why our support starts with finding an employer willing to sponsor you.

The main ones are: the salaried worker visa (permanent contract), the temporary worker visa (fixed-term contract), the "worker" residence card and the talent passport for qualified profiles. We determine the most suitable solution for your situation.

The delay varies by sector and visa type, but generally allow 2 to 6 months. We give you a realistic schedule from the first appointment.

Depending on the visa type and after a certain period, family reunification may be possible. We inform you precisely about your rights and the procedures related to family.

Our prices are transparent and shared during the free first appointment. They depend on the complexity of the file. No hidden fees: you know exactly what is included.

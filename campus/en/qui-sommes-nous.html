Our mission

Your success in Europe starts here

Campus Inter is an agency specialized in supporting mobility projects to Europe. We help every candidate fulfill their dream with complete peace of mind.

Clarifying your project

Identify your goals, desires and possibilities from the first appointment.

Choosing the right path

Directing you toward the program or path best suited to your profile.

Succeeding in your procedures

Build a solid file and follow every administrative step with rigor.

Settling in with peace of mind

Supporting you on site: accommodation, formalities and successful integration.

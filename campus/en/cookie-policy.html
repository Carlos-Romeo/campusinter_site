Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies on our website.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. It allows the site to store information about your browsing and to recognize you during future visits.

3. Cookie Purposes

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Website operation: ensuring security, navigation, and form usage
  • Audience measurement: analyzing traffic and improving user experience
  • Preferences: remembering your choices (language, cookie consent)

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can modify your cookie preferences at any time:

  • Cookie banner: on your first visit, you can accept or decline non-essential cookies
  • Browser settings: you can configure your browser to block or delete cookies
How to disable cookies in your browser:

• Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Cookies
• Firefox: Options → Privacy → Cookies
• Safari → Preferences → Privacy
• Edge → Settings → Privacy → Cookies

5. Third-party Cookies

Our website may contain embedded content (YouTube videos, social sharing buttons). These third-party services may place cookies that we do not control. We invite you to consult their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics: Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube: Google Privacy Policy
  • Font Awesome (CDN): no cookies placed
  • Google Fonts: Google Privacy Policy

6. Retention Period

Cookies have varying retention periods depending on their purpose (see tables above). Beyond these periods, data is automatically deleted.

7. Your Rights

In accordance with GDPR, you have rights regarding your personal data collected via cookies. For more information, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

This cookie policy may be updated at any time. The date of the last update is indicated at the top of this page.
